# AI Compute Demand Supercycle

Training and inference compute demand is doubling every ~18 months. No existing fab capacity can meet this without multi-year buildout. Sovereign AI programs (UAE, Saudi, France) are adding a new demand layer independent of US hyperscalers.

## Why Durable
Foundation model scaling laws remain intact. Enterprise AI adoption just beginning — most Fortune 500s are still in pilot phase. Sovereign compute buildouts require domestic infrastructure that cannot be rented from US clouds.
