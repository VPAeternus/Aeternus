## Signal Architecture Principle

**Open/close signals only for alpha generation. Intraday monitoring for risk only.**

Intraday noise adds no alpha and increases cost. Our signals are calculated on open and close data — that is where the edge lives. However, the world moves intraday. When Trump posts, when a Fed speaker deviates, when a sector-defining announcement drops — positions must be risk-managed in real time.

Two layers:
1. **Signal layer** — daily, overnight, open/close. This is where alpha is generated.
2. **Risk sentinel** — continuous, lightweight, event-driven. This is where capital is protected.

Never conflate the two.

## The Two-Path Model

Most emerging managers choose one path: build privately, approach allocators, raise a fund. That path is correct — but it leaves 3 years of zero external revenue while building on personal capital. It also means your track record is a private PDF that allocators have no way to independently verify.

We run both paths simultaneously. They are not in conflict. They are synergistic.

### Path 1: The Institutional Track Record (Private, Audited)

- Live trading with real capital from day one
- Every decision logged, attributed, auditable
- Third-party verification over time
- The traditional path to family offices and institutional allocators

### Path 2: The Subscription Float (Public, Transparent)

- Same positions, same reasoning — published to subscribers
- Monthly subscription MRR from day one of live trading
- The Buffett float model: collect premiums today, compound capital tomorrow
- Even if returns are flat in a quarter, operating capital flows in

**The float is the key insight.** A fund manager charging 1.5% on AUM needs $10M AUM to generate $150K in management fees. A subscription portfolio needs 1,000 followers at $30/month to generate the same. And those 1,000 followers are also a pre-qualified investor pipeline for when the fund launches.

## The Content Strategy Principle

**Publish the actual process. Not a marketing version of it.**

Every trade our system makes is accompanied by a full reasoning chain: the fundamental score breakdown, the bull case, the bear case, the coherence analysis, the conviction level, the uncertainty. That IS the content. It goes to subscribers in the same form it was generated — including the uncertainty, including the debate, including when the bear case was close.

This is what no human portfolio manager can produce consistently. A human PM writes 2-3 hours of threads explaining a trade after the fact. Our system generates the reasoning as a byproduct of making the decision. The marginal cost of content production is near zero.

What this builds: an audience that trusts the process, not the personality. That audience does not churn when returns are flat for a quarter because they understand why the decision was made. That audience converts to fund investors because they have seen the discipline over time.

What this does not do: optimize for engagement. Hot takes, confident predictions, and entertainment build followers who leave when the next exciting PM arrives. We do not chase that audience.
