# Venture Mode archetypes

Venture Mode is one generic framework.
It is not a `BE-only` framework.

## Two valid internal win paths

| Path | Core question | Typical winner shape | What matters most |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Expansion Bridge` | Can this smaller-base company bridge into scale early enough to catch the wave? | emerging wave rider | financing, policy, partners, deployment runway |
| `Torque Bottleneck` | Is this already-real supplier sitting in a bottleneck layer where wave demand can torque earnings power much faster than the market assumes? | underappreciated enabling supplier | installed position, utilization recovery, mix shift, margin torque, demand acceleration |

## Expansion Bridge
Use when the filing shows:
- explicit new wave attachment
- much larger upside from a relatively small base
- credible bridge to survival and scale
- partner, policy, deployment, or financing support that materially changes viability

This is the path that matched the early `BE` read.

## Torque Bottleneck
Use when the filing shows:
- direct role in an enabling or bottleneck layer of the wave
- active AI / cloud / infrastructure demand already lifting the business
- strong operating leverage if volumes, utilization, or mix improve
- underappreciated upside even though the company is already established

This is the path LITE was trying to qualify through after packet repair.

## Important guardrail
- `established` does not automatically mean `saturated`
- maturity becomes a penalty only when the filing mostly confirms upside that is already obvious
- a mature supplier can still qualify if the filing shows underappreciated wave torque

## Practical use
For each Venture Mode winner, write one extra line:
- `Archetype path: Expansion Bridge`
or
- `Archetype path: Torque Bottleneck`
