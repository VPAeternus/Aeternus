# LITE framework blind spots review

| Area | What the filings showed | What the framework rewarded | What the market may have rewarded faster |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI relevance | Real cloud / AI-ML / data-center interconnect exposure | Moderate wave exposure | Faster repricing of optical-AI leverage |
| Asymmetry | Established optical leader, not tiny base | Lower asymmetry than `BE` | Operating leverage from AI demand inflection |
| Filing delta | Strongest repaired evidence mostly in `2025-08-19 10-K` | Some improvement, but not breakaway | Market may have moved ahead of explicit filing delta |
| Scale bridge | Good liquidity, but less dramatic financing scaffold than `BE` | Solid, not exceptional | Investors may not have needed a `Brookfield/AEP`-style bridge |
| Penalties | Commoditization, margin pressure, inventory normalization, China/export friction | Meaningful drag | Market may have looked through these as temporary |

## Blind spots
1. The framework is too dependent on **explicit narrative novelty**.
   - LITE may win when the filing mostly confirms a setup the market can already re-rate aggressively.
   - That hurts names where the upside comes from operating leverage, not from a dramatic new disclosed partnership.

2. The framework over-rewards **small-base asymmetry** versus **high-beta bottleneck suppliers**.
   - `BE` fits `small base + giant upside` perfectly.
   - `LITE` fits `critical enabler of a huge buildout with strong torque`.
   - Current Venture Mode favors the first archetype too hard.

3. The framework treats **maturity** too much like **saturation**.
   - LITE is mature in category history.
   - But that does not mean the AI optical cycle was fully priced.
   - A mature supplier can still be an underappreciated wave torque play.

4. The framework underweights **AI demand operating leverage**.
   - Repaired LITE filings show:
     - cloud data center operators
     - AI/ML infrastructure providers
     - data center interconnect
     - increasing demand from AI and cloud customers
   - But the scoring still lacks a clean factor for: `existing supplier with strong earnings torque if AI demand accelerates`.

5. The framework is too harsh on **optical commoditization language**.
   - In optical, companies often disclose commoditization risk structurally even during strong cycle turns.
   - That may be a permanent background risk, not a timing signal.

## Candidate framework fix
Add a new Venture Mode adjustment:
- `Wave Torque / Operating Leverage (0-3)`

Reward when filings show all or most of:
- direct participation in a scaling bottleneck layer of the wave
- existing product fit into active AI / cloud deployment
- evidence demand is already improving from the wave
- installed manufacturing / commercial position that can convert volume quickly
- upside can come from margin / mix / utilization recovery, not only from new financing scaffolds

And narrow the maturity penalty:
- do **not** penalize a company just for being established
- penalize only when the filing shows the upside is already obvious and mostly continuation

## Practical interpretation
- `BE` is still the better `venture-foundation hypergrowth` archetype.
- `LITE` may be the better `AI infrastructure torque rerating` archetype.
- Those are not the same thing.

## Recommendation
Do not force one Venture Mode to cover both:
1. `venture-foundation wave rider` (`BE` type)
2. `underappreciated bottleneck supplier torque play` (`LITE` type)

If you want LITE-style winners to surface, add a second sub-mode or a new factor for `wave torque / operating leverage`.
